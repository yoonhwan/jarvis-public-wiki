1-bit LLM

Bonsai 27B, shrinked from 54GB to just 3.8GB (-93%), runs locally in your browser.

  • Models: https://huggingface.co/collections/prism-ml/bonsai-27b
  • Demo: https://huggingface.co/spaces/webml-community/bonsai-webgpu-kernels
