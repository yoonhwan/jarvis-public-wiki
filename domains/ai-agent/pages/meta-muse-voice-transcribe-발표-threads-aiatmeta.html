Meta Muse Voice Transcribe 발표 (Threads @aiatmeta)

🇰🇷 한글 요약

Meta Superintelligence Labs가 Muse Voice Transcribe를 공개했다 — 실시간 음성 인식(ASR)을 수행하는 첫 번째 real-time audio perception 모델.

기술 원리 (원문 스레드 기반)

  1. 스트리밍 ASR 기반 구조: 오디오를 80ms 단위 청크로 나눠 각 청크를 하나의 soft token으로 변환. 모델은 각 청크마다 <|nextaudio|> 토큰(더 듣기) 또는 텍스트 토큰(출력) 중 선택. 오디오 스트림이 끝나면 <|emptyaudio|> 토큰을 삽입해 남은 텍스트를 모두 출력.
  2. Adaptive Delay (적응형 지연): RL(word error rate + delay reward 결합)로 학습되어, 어려운 단어에서는 더 오래 기다리고 쉬운 단어에서는 빨리 출력 — 단어 단위로 정확도-지연시간 트레이드오프를 조절. 이 덕분에 "최종 전사까지 걸리는 시간" 기준 speed-accuracy pareto frontier(최적 균형점)를 달성.
  3. Diarization + Endpointing: 스트리밍 ASR 위에 특수 토큰을 추가해 확장. 화자 전환 감지용 <|startofturn|> 토큰과 화자 구분용 <|speaker_{A-Z}|> 태그를 사용. 화자 전환은 즉시 예측하되, 화자 태그 예측은 청크 끝까지 지연시켜 정확도를 높임.

How It Works (from the original thread)

  1. Streaming ASR foundation: Audio is processed in 80ms chunks, each transformed into a single soft token. At every chunk, the model predicts either a <|nextaudio|> token (keep listening) or emits text. When the stream ends, an <|emptyaudio|> token signals no more audio, and the model flushes all remaining text.
  2. Adaptive delay: Trained with RL combining word error rate and delay rewards, the model waits longer on hard words and commits sooner on easy ones — trading accuracy against latency word by word. This lets it hit the Pareto frontier on speed-accuracy trade-off measured by time-to-final-transcription.
  3. Diarization + endpointing on top of ASR: Built by adding special tokens on top of the streaming ASR backbone — <|startofturn|> to flag a speaker switch (predicted immediately) and <|speaker_{A-Z}|> tags to differentiate speakers (prediction delayed to end of chunk for accuracy).

Notable Replies

  • A user tested mixed Vietnamese+English speech: impressed by speed/accuracy, suggested two improvements — (1) better handling of code-switching when foreign words are spoken with a local accent, (2) catching quieter/faster words squeezed between others.

원문 스레드 (Author's Thread, 4 posts)

  1. (Main post) Introducing Muse Voice Transcribe, the first real-time audio perception model from Meta Superintelligence Labs. Delivers real-time streaming ASR, diarization with 20+ speakers, and endpointing. Multilingual with seamless code-switching and improves accuracy with language, keyword, and context biasing. Ranks first on Artificial Analysis streaming speech-to-text and on public diarization benchmarks.
  2. Muse Voice Transcribe is an autoregressive multimodal LLM from the Muse Spark family. Audio is processed in 80ms chunks (12.5 Hz), one token each, and at every chunk the model decides whether to keep listening or emit text. RL with combined word error rate and delay rewards gives it adaptive delay: it waits longer on hard words and commits sooner on easy ones, trading accuracy against latency word by word.
  3. With adaptive delay, Muse Voice Transcribe achieves the pareto frontier on speed-accuracy trade-off measured by time to final transcription.
  4. Muse Voice Transcribe is available today via Meta Model API, Meta AI for Mac, and Muse Code. Learn more: https://go.meta.me/4xISnl2 → 리다이렉트: https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-voice-transcribe
